Understanding Machine Learning and LLMs

This lesson will help you grasp the basics of how machine learning models, specifically large language models (LLMs), function and how they can be utilized effectively.

In 60 Seconds

  • Machine learning uses algorithms to learn from data and make predictions or decisions.
  • Large Language Models (LLMs) are a type of machine learning model trained on vast text datasets to understand and generate human-like language.
  • Understanding how to use LLMs with additional data can enhance their effectiveness in real-world applications.
LEARN

Today's Lesson

Machine learning (ML) is a subset of artificial intelligence that enables systems to learn from data without being explicitly programmed. It involves algorithms that analyze data, find patterns, and make predictions. This process can be applied to various tasks, from recommending products to recognizing speech.

Among the different types of models, large language models (LLMs) stand out due to their ability to process and generate natural language. LLMs are built on deep learning, a branch of machine learning that employs neural networks with multiple layers to capture complex relationships in data.

To enhance LLMs, businesses often use a technique known as Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). This approach combines the power of LLMs with additional relevant data, allowing them to provide more accurate and context-aware responses. By integrating their own datasets, organizations boost the models' performance and relevance.

Example: If a company has a knowledge base of FAQs, they can feed this data into a RAG pipeline to improve responses generated by an LLM in customer support chats.
