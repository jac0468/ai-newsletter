Prompt Lab

Weak Prompt: "Tell me about AI."

Better Prompt: "As an AI expert, provide a brief overview of how AI is impacting productivity in the workplace, with examples of tools that are widely used."

Why It Works: The better prompt specifies a role (AI expert), context (impact on productivity), task (overview), constraints (brief), and desired output (examples of tools), guiding the AI to generate a more focused and informative response.
