Prompt Lab

Weak Prompt: "Tell me about AI."

Better Prompt: "As an AI expert, summarize the key applications of AI in business today, focusing on practical examples and limitations."

Why It Works: The better prompt sets a clear role (AI expert), specifies the focus (business applications), and outlines what you want (examples and limitations), increasing the chances of getting a useful response.
