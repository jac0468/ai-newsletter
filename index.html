Demystifying AI: Understanding Models and Machine Learning

By the end of this lesson, you will understand the basic concepts of artificial intelligence, including models, large language models (LLMs), and the principles of machine learning.

In 60 Seconds

  • AI models are algorithms designed to perform specific tasks by learning from data.
  • Large language models (LLMs) process and generate text based on vast amounts of written data.
  • Machine learning is the technique that enables models to improve their performance over time through experience.
LEARN

Today's Lesson

At its core, AI consists of algorithms called models that learn from data to perform tasks. These models can be thought of as educated guesses based on patterns found in historical data. For example, an AI model might learn to recognize images or generate text by analyzing thousands of examples.

Large language models (LLMs) are a specific type of model that focus on understanding and generating human language. They are trained on extensive datasets, allowing them to understand context and produce relevant text. However, to maximize their usefulness, they often need to incorporate additional information or specific data through techniques like retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

Machine learning, the backbone of AI, is a technique used to improve model performance without explicit programming. Instead of hardcoding rules, machine learning relies on feeding data into the model, allowing it to identify patterns and make predictions. Over time, as the model encounters more data, it refines its approach and becomes more accurate.

Example: A chatbot that uses an LLM can learn to answer customer questions by analyzing previous conversations to determine the best responses.
