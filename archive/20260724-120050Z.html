Prompt Lab

Weak Prompt: "Tell me about climate change."

Better Prompt: "As an environmental scientist, can you summarize the major causes and effects of climate change in simple terms?"

Why It Works: The better prompt gives clear roles (environmental scientist), context (climate change), and specifics (major causes and effects) that direct the AI to provide a focused and informative answer.
