Prompt Lab

Weak Prompt: "Tell me about AI."

Better Prompt: "As an AI expert, please summarize the key benefits and challenges of using AI in business settings."

Why It Works: The better prompt specifies the role (AI expert), context (business settings), and desired output (summary of benefits and challenges), making it easier for the AI to deliver a focused and relevant response.
