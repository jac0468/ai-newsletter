Today's Lesson

Claude is designed to help you build efficient, reusable AI applications. By managing your projects effectively and creating reusable skills, you can save time and effort in your daily tasks.

First, you need to understand Claude projects. They are the foundation of your reusable skills. Using a well-structured project can make it easier for Claude to help you with tasks over time, as your project will define its context and capabilities.

Next, leverage CLAUDE.md files. These files store essential instructions and memory for your projects. By strategically organizing your files, you can ensure Claude retains the information it needs to assist you accurately.

Example: Suppose you create a project called "Weekly Reports" that includes scripts for generating summaries based on data input. Once set up, you can reuse this project for future reports with minimal adjustments.
