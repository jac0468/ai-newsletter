Understanding the Basics of AI Models and LLMs

By the end of this lesson, you will better understand the fundamental concepts behind machine learning, models, and large language models (LLMs) to become a more effective AI user.

In 60 Seconds

  • Models are mathematical representations of data used to make predictions.

  • Machine learning involves teaching models to improve over time with data.
LEARN

Today's Lesson

At its core, AI revolves around models, which are like sophisticated mathematical formulas used to analyze data. These models learn from training data to predict outcomes. For example, a model could be used to determine whether an email is spam based on its contents.

Large language models (LLMs) are a specific type of model designed to process and generate text. They analyze vast amounts of text data to understand grammar, facts, and even some common sense reasoning, allowing them to produce coherent and contextually appropriate responses.

Machine learning (ML) is the broader field that encompasses how models learn from data. It's about programming computers to recognize patterns and make decisions with minimal human intervention. Over time, as they receive more data, these models refine their predictions and improve accuracy.

Example: Think of an LLM like a smart assistant that can write an email for you. You give it some context, and it generates text based on what it has learned from a massive amount of similar content.
