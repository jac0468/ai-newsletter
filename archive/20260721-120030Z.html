Prompt Lab

Weak Prompt: "Tell me about using AI."

Better Prompt: "As a productivity expert, can you outline three specific ways I can use AI tools to enhance my daily workflow?"

Why It Works: The better prompt gives the AI more context (my role), specifies the request (ways to enhance workflow), and defines the output I want, leading to a more relevant and actionable answer.
