Today's Lesson

Choosing the right AI tool starts with understanding what each tool specializes in. For instance, some platforms excel in drafting content, while others are tailored for research or summarization. By knowing these strengths, you can leverage the right AI solution for your tasks.

One essential strategy is to tailor your prompts according to the platform's strengths. If you're using ChatGPT for brainstorming, your inquiries should be open-ended to yield a variety of ideas. In contrast, if you're working with Perplexity for data sourcing, your questions should be specific and focused.

Take the time to experiment with a few different tools for the same task. You might find that Gemini provides better results for Google-related inquiries, whereas Claude might be more effective for long-form writing. Consistent practice will make you a more effective collaborator with AI.

Example: If you need to generate a research topic, try ChatGPT first for creative ideas, then refine your question in Perplexity to gather data.
