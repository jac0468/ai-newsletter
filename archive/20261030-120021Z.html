Today's Lesson

AI models are essentially algorithms that learn from data to perform specific tasks. They analyze patterns in data to make predictions, classify information, or generate new content. When it comes to large language models (LLMs), these are specialized AI models trained to understand and generate text that resembles human conversation.

Machine learning, a key component of AI, involves training models using large datasets. For instance, a machine learning model can learn to recognize images of cats by being shown thousands of cat photos. The more data the model receives, the better it becomes at identifying cats in new images.

To be effective, AI models need clean, relevant data. The quality of the training data directly impacts how well the model performs. In practical applications, you will often work with pre-trained models that have already learned from extensive datasets, allowing you to use them without starting from scratch.

Example: A beginner could use services like chatbots powered by LLMs to assist in customer support, leveraging their ability to understand and generate human-like responses.
